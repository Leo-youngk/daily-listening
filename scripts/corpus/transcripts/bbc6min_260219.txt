Chantel sent her CV to the world's top restaurants. CV is short for curriculum vitae, a document listing someone's education, qualifications and work experience, which they write when looking for a job. In American English, it's called a résumé.
Chantel applied to only Michelin-star-winning restaurants. She aimed high, meaning she was ambitious. Chantel says, " The worst they could do is say no," a phrase which means you shouldn't be afraid of trying to do something because the worst outcome is being rejected.

At the age of 26, Chantel left London and returned home to open her own restaurant, Mosaic, near Pretoria in South Africa.
